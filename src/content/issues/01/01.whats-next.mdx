Time's moving by fast, so fast. It feels like the end of the world. The sirens
have been ringing for a while, but these days they feel especially loud. The
Nothing Ever Happens crowd is usually triumphant until those moments that change
history. That's just how it works. For something to be eventful it must happen
sparingly. Until all of a sudden — surprise!

Then everything changes.
